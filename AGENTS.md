# AGENTS.md

Before changing this repository:

1. Re-read `README.md`, `docs/SCOPE.md`, `docs/SCHEMA.md`, and `docs/ROADMAP.md`.
2. Re-audit latest `main`, open PRs/issues, Actions, and the target branch head.
3. Treat canonical data as reviewed publication data. Do not promote monitoring or research notes directly into canonical records.
4. Keep program, provider, relation, event, and evidence boundaries separate.
5. Do not infer that a program was affected by an infrastructure incident merely because it used the same provider. Record explicit affected/unaffected/unknown evidence separately.
6. Do not treat confidence as a safety score or product recommendation.
7. Preserve uncertainty. Unknown is preferable to an unsupported assertion.
8. Normal code/docs/data PRs may proceed without production deployment. Production mutation/deployment requires the repository's applicable authorization policy once one exists.
