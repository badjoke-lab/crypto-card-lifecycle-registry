# Record Growth Policy

## Goal

Grow the registry with evidence-backed historical records, not a thin card directory.

## Promotion gate

A canonical program/provider record should be promoted only when:

1. the entity is clearly in scope;
2. an official or otherwise reliable identity source is captured;
3. material provider relationships are recorded when known and evidenced;
4. material lifecycle events are recorded when they are part of why the entity matters historically;
5. uncertainty is preserved rather than filled with inference;
6. canonical validation passes.

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer, in order:

1. official statements, documentation, terms, regulatory filings;
2. on-chain or independently reproducible technical evidence;
3. reputable independent reporting;
4. archives of otherwise unavailable primary material;
5. social/community reports as leads, not as automatic canonical truth.

A lower-ranked source may still be used when it directly supports a narrow claim and stronger evidence is unavailable, but confidence must reflect that limitation.

## Provider/program boundary

A program must not inherit an incident state from a provider merely because a relationship exists.

Affected, unaffected, suspended, reimbursed, migrated, or restored states require program-specific evidence when asserted at the program level.

Likewise, a program incident does not automatically become a provider-wide incident.

## No thin-count growth

Do not add a large number of card names with only homepage URLs and no useful lifecycle context.

Prioritize records that add historical or structural value, including:

- launch or discontinuation;
- issuer/processor/provider changes;
- custody/collateral/settlement changes;
- service limitations or suspensions;
- exploits and unauthorized withdrawals;
- reimbursement or recovery milestones;
- regulatory actions;
- migrations and shutdowns.

## Batch discipline

Use bounded review batches. Each batch should state:

- candidate set;
- accepted canonical records;
- rejected/out-of-scope candidates;
- unresolved candidates;
- evidence gaps;
- validator result.

Monitoring/research output remains staging until reviewed.

## Cross-registry boundary

Do not duplicate a crypto-card incident into another Ledger Series registry unless that incident changes the canonical lifecycle/state of an entity genuinely covered by that registry.
