# Phase 3 batch 009 review — WaveCrest shutdown generations

Date: 2026-08-30

## Candidate set

- TenX Card (WaveCrest-issued generation)
- Bitwala Card (WaveCrest-issued generation)
- BitPay Card Europe / international non-U.S. WaveCrest generation
- Xapo Card and other WaveCrest-dependent programs as follow-up candidates

## Accepted

### TenX Card (WaveCrest generation)

TenX's official September 2017 update identifies Wave Crest Holdings Limited as issuer of every active TenX card then in circulation and documents the 2017-10-16 loss of usability for cardholders outside the European territory. TenX's January 2018 update then states that WaveCrest disabled its cards after an urgent card-network advisory, causing the remaining WaveCrest-issued TenX cards to stop working.

This is modeled as a discontinued historical card generation rather than implying that every later TenX card generation ended on the same date.

### Bitwala Card (WaveCrest generation)

Bitwala's own retrospective says Visa abruptly terminated its relationship with WaveCrest in 2018 and that Bitwala was forced to halt its debit-card offering. This historical generation is separated from later Bitwala/Nuri and current Bitwala card products.

### BitPay Card (Europe, WaveCrest generation)

A contemporaneous report reproduces BitPay's January 2018 statement that its European card was no longer available from issuer Wave Crest Holdings Ltd and explicitly notes that U.S. BitPay Card users were unaffected. Because the original historical BitPay post is not currently retrievable, this record remains medium confidence rather than being promoted to high-confidence first-party status.

## Shared provider

Wave Crest Holdings Limited is added once as the historical issuer dependency. Program impact is still recorded separately for each program; the shared provider relationship is not used to infer effects on unreviewed programs.

## Deferred / unresolved

- Xapo Card: the 2018 closure is well-attested in contemporaneous reproductions of Xapo's customer notice, but a currently retrievable first-party source has not yet been secured.
- additional WaveCrest programs: do not bulk-promote names from a shared shutdown list without program-specific evidence.

## Boundary decisions

- no UI changes
- no blanket inference that every crypto card using WaveCrest is canonical-affected without program-specific evidence
- the TenX 2017 non-Europe restriction is a separate regional limitation event from the January 2018 shutdown
- later replacement/relaunch card generations are not silently merged into the reviewed WaveCrest generation
- BitPay Europe remains medium confidence because the strongest currently retrievable source is contemporaneous reporting reproducing BitPay's statement
