# Phase 3 batch 016 research — 2026-09-02

## Bounded candidate set

This batch continues reviewed canonical record growth with three card programs that are not present in the current canonical program set.

### 1. 1inch Card

First-party evidence:

- 1inch, `1inch launches Web3 crypto debit card with Mastercard`, 2024-04-08 (updated 2026-05-08): https://1inch.com/blog/post/1inch-launches-a-web3-debit-card-in-partnership-with-mastercard-and-crypto-life
- 1inch Help Center, `1inch Card — Basic Overview`: https://help.1inch.com/en/articles/13127532-1inch-card-basic-overview
- 1inch Terms of Use, current document: https://1inch.com/assets/1inch_com_terms_of_use.pdf

Supported claims:

- 1inch launched a physical and virtual crypto debit card in 2024.
- The card uses Mastercard and converts crypto to fiat at payment time.
- Current 1inch terms identify Monavate Limited as issuer of the prepaid Mastercard and identify the 1inch CL Card as powered by Crypto Life; Baanx Group Ltd is named among service providers.
- Current availability is primarily in eligible EU countries and remains jurisdiction-dependent.

Canonical decision:

- Promote as a distinct active program.
- Reuse existing Mastercard, Monavate, Crypto Life and Baanx provider records rather than duplicate them.
- Scope issuer/provider relations only as far as the first-party terms support.

### 2. Kraken Card

First-party evidence:

- Kraken, `Your Kraken balance, ready to spend: the Kraken Card is here`, 2026-07-13: https://blog.kraken.com/product/kraken-card/introducing-the-kraken-card
- Kraken product page: https://www.kraken.com/features/crypto-card
- Kraken Support, `Getting Started with Kraken Card`, updated 2026-07-20: https://support.kraken.com/articles/getting-started-kraken-card

Supported claims:

- Kraken Card launched on 2026-07-13 for eligible customers in the United Kingdom and EEA.
- It is a Mastercard debit card available in virtual and physical form and can spend supported crypto and fiat balances.
- Kraken identifies UAB Monavate as issuer under a Mastercard licence.

Canonical decision:

- Promote as a distinct active program.
- Reuse existing Mastercard and Monavate provider records.
- Scope launch and issuer/network relations to UK/EEA rather than generalizing future market expansion.

### 3. Coinbase One Card

First-party evidence:

- Coinbase product page: https://www.coinbase.com/creditcard
- Coinbase Help, `Coinbase One Card (credit) - Overview`: https://help.coinbase.com/en/creditcard/overview
- Coinbase, `Earn up to 4% bitcoin back on every purchase with the new Coinbase One Card`, 2025-06-12: https://www.coinbase.com/blog/earn-up-to-4-percent-bitcoin-back-on-every-purchase-with-the-new-coinbase-one-card
- American Express, `New Coinbase One Card to Launch on the American Express Network`, 2025-06-12: https://ir.americanexpress.com/news/investor-relations-news/investor-relations-news-details/2025/New-Coinbase-One-Card-to-Launch-on-the-American-Express-Network/default.aspx

Supported claims:

- Coinbase announced the Coinbase One Card on 2025-06-12 and the current product is available to eligible Coinbase One members in the United States.
- It is a credit card that earns bitcoin-denominated rewards rather than a debit card that directly spends crypto balances.
- Current product disclosures identify First Electronic Bank as issuer, Cardless as a program partner/provider, and the American Express network.

Canonical decision:

- Promote as a distinct active program from the existing Coinbase debit-card record and historical Shift generation.
- Add First Electronic Bank, Cardless and American Express as providers only with roles directly supported by first-party disclosures.
- Treat 2025-06-12 as the public announcement milestone, not an invented exact first-transaction or full-rollout date.

## Promotion gate

- Preserve the distinction between debit cards that spend/convert crypto and rewards credit cards tied to a crypto platform.
- Every relation evidence object must identify both endpoints.
- Every event evidence object must identify the program.
- Do not infer narrower processor/program-manager roles where the source only states a partnership/provider relationship.
- Run the repository validation workflow before merge.
