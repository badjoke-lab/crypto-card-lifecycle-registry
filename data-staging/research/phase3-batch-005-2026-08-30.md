## Accepted

### Bybit EU Card

Accepted as a distinct program from the legacy/current Bybit Card on bybit.com.

Primary evidence reviewed:
- https://www.bybit.eu/en-EU/help-center/article/Introduction-to-Bybit-Card?category=0bc68854116cfb83d2 — Bybit EU identifies its card as a Mastercard debit card using the Bybit EU Funding Account.
- https://www.bybit.eu/en-EU/learn/bybit-card/how-to-get-started-bybit-card-bybit-eu — current availability in select EEA countries plus Switzerland, Liechtenstein and Norway; Mastercard merchant/network usage.
- https://www.bybit.com/en/help-center/article/FAQ-Changes-to-Bybit-Card-Services-in-the-EEA-CH-region — legacy bybit.com EEA/CH users were directed to apply for a new, separate Bybit EU Card and to complete transition no later than 2025-12-31.

Canonical promotion:
- distinct Bybit EU Card program
- Mastercard network relation scoped to Bybit EU eligible European markets
- `migrated_to` relation from legacy Bybit Card to Bybit EU Card for EEA/Switzerland
- existing migration evidence expanded to cover both relation endpoints

Unresolved and intentionally not inferred:
- exact issuer/legal entity for the current Bybit EU Card was not established by the reviewed public material and is not added.
- the 2025-12-31 date is a recommended transition deadline, not proof every holder migrated on that date.

### Wirex Card

Accepted after resolving the regional infrastructure gap that caused deferral in batch 004.

Primary evidence reviewed:
- https://help.wirexapp.com/it/article/mastercard-issuer-terms-new-eea-1556 — EEA terms identify Transact Payments Malta Limited as issuer, Wirex UAB as Program Manager, Mastercard as scheme, and eligibility in the EEA excluding Cyprus and Liechtenstein.
- https://help.wirexapp.com/article/terms-and-conditions-united-kingdom-1598 — UK general terms state the Contis Financial Services partnership ended on 2021-03-16 and refer to the old single-currency Visa Card.
- https://help.wirexapp.com/article/issuer-terms-singapore-1581 — Singapore terms identify Wirex Pte Ltd as Card Issuer.

Canonical promotion:
- Wirex Card program
- Transact Payments Malta Limited issuer provider
- Wirex UAB program-manager provider
- Wirex Pte Ltd issuer provider
- EEA issuer/program-manager/Mastercard relations with explicit regional exclusion note
- Singapore issuer relation
- 2021-03-16 UK provider-change lifecycle event recording the end of the Contis partnership and legacy Visa-card context

Uncertainty preserved:
- the Contis source supports the partnership end and legacy Visa-card context but does not establish a replacement provider in the same statement; no replacement provider is inferred.
- current Visa/Mastercard memberships outside the modeled EEA/Singapore relationships are not generalized globally.
