# Phase 3 batch 021 research — 2026-09-08

## Candidate set

1. Karta Personal Spend Card
2. Stablecard by Western Union
3. SPay Card

## Decision

All three candidates are accepted for canonical promotion. Canonical duplicate searches returned no existing program match for Karta, Stablecard by Western Union, or SPay.

## 1. Karta Personal Spend Card — accepted

First-party evidence reviewed:
- Karta current product page: https://karta.io/
- Karta B2C Card Terms: https://legal.karta.io/b2c-card-terms
- Karta Account Opening Privacy Notice: https://legal.karta.io/account-opening-privacy-notice

Supported claims:
- self-custodial stablecoin-funded Visa Signature spending card;
- virtual and physical card formats;
- current card terms identify Third National as issuer and Visa as network;
- digital-asset collateral remains in the linked wallet structure described by the terms;
- current reviewed terms are effective 2026-05-05.

Boundary / uncertainty:
- 2026-05-05 is treated as a current legal/infrastructure evidence milestone, not asserted as the original product launch date or exact issuer cutover date.

## 2. Stablecard by Western Union — accepted

First-party evidence reviewed:
- Western Union Stablecard product page: https://www.westernunion.com/global/en/digital-assets-stablecard.html
- Western Union launch release: https://ir.westernunion.com/news/archived-press-releases/press-release-details/2026/Western-Union-Launches-Stablecard-in-Partnership-with-Rain/default.aspx
- Stablecard international consumer card terms: https://westernunionstablecardlegal.com/international-consumer-card-terms

Supported claims:
- Western Union launched Stablecard on 2026-08-04;
- USDPT-backed secured Visa card and associated digital wallet;
- Western Union identifies Rain as card issuer and program manager for the current product disclosure;
- current legal terms separately identify Third National as issuer under Visa licence;
- USDPT is issued by Anchorage Digital Bank on Solana, but Anchorage is collateral-asset context rather than a card-provider relation in this batch.

Boundary / uncertainty:
- Rain and Third National wording is preserved rather than collapsed into a new legal-entity inference. Existing Rain and ThirdNational provider records are reused according to the direct first-party wording.
- Western Union is not assigned a regulated issuer/lender role.

## 3. SPay Card — accepted

First-party evidence reviewed:
- SPay current product page: https://spay.finance/
- SPay Privacy Policy: https://spay.finance/privacy-policy/

Supported claims:
- stablecoin wallet and USD Visa card product;
- current product supports USDT/USDC funding and merchant settlement over Visa;
- current SPay disclosure identifies Third National as card issuer under licence from Visa;
- SPay identifies NOVARA TECH L.L.C S.O.C. as operator of the service.

Boundary / uncertainty:
- no exact original card launch date was established from the reviewed first-party evidence, so no launch event is created;
- NOVARA TECH L.L.C S.O.C. is not assigned a narrower issuer/program-manager role without explicit evidence.
