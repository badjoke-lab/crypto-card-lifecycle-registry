## Bitget Wallet Card

Accepted as distinct from legacy Bitget Card (`ccr_program_000029`).

First-party evidence:
- https://web3.bitget.com/card — current Bitget Wallet Card product page; crypto payment card across multiple regions, Visa/Mastercard depending on regional issuing partner.
- https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603889935 — new Bitget Card applications moved to Bitget Wallet from 2026-08-01 while existing Bitget Cards remained active; establishes product distinction and transition boundary.
- https://web3.bitget.com/en/blog/articles/bitget-wallet-card-africa — South Africa rollout; Mastercard and Immersve, self-custodial wallet spending.
- https://web3.bitget.com/ja/helpCenter/235 — Mainland China card documentation identifying Fiat24 partnership.

Provider handling:
- reuse Mastercard (`ccr_provider_000005`)
- reuse Visa (`ccr_provider_000006`)
- reuse Fiat24 (`ccr_provider_000039`) only for the evidenced Mainland China scope
- Immersve is not added in this bounded batch because the Africa page supports a regional provider role but the canonical provider-type mapping needs a separate identity/legal-role review before promotion.

Uncertainty preserved:
- no single global issuer is assigned because Bitget explicitly says issuing partners vary by region.
- the legacy Bitget Card is not marked shut down; existing cards continue until expiry and cannot be renewed after 2026-08-01.
