# Phase 3 batch 020 research — 2026-09-04

Bounded candidate set:
1. Lemon Card (Argentina prepaid generation)
2. Lemon Bitcoin-backed Credit Card
3. Takenos TakeCard

## Lemon Card — Argentina prepaid generation

Accepted.

First-party evidence reviewed:
- Digifin prepaid-card terms: https://digifin.ghost.io/terminos-y-condiciones-tarjeta-prepaga/
  - Digifin S.A. offers and issues the physical/virtual Tarjeta Prepaga Lemon.
  - Visa network in Argentina and abroad.
  - Product is explicitly prepaid, not debit or credit.
  - Current account balance in Argentine pesos backs spending; Lemon platform also supports crypto conversion through Bay Treasure Caramel.
- Lemon help: https://help.lemon.me/es/articles/4868375-que-es-la-lemon-card-y-donde-la-puedo-usar
  - Current Argentina Lemon Card remains a prepaid Visa card and can be funded with pesos or crypto through the Lemon experience.
- Lemon historical product article: https://wiki.lemon.me/es-ar/lemon-cash-app/lemon-card
  - Published 2021-11-08 and presents the Lemon Card as the Argentina Visa card launch generation.
- Lemon historical/current cashback article: https://wiki.lemon.me/es-ar/novedades/como-funciona-el-cashback-de-la-lemon-card
  - States Lemon Card launched in November 2021.

Decision:
- Add active Lemon Card (Argentina prepaid) as its own program.
- Add Digifin S.A. as issuer.
- Reuse Visa network.
- Add 2021-11-08 launch event based on first-party launch article date, while noting broader first-party retrospective only fixes the month as November 2021.

## Lemon Bitcoin-backed Credit Card

Accepted as a distinct program from the prepaid Lemon Card.

First-party evidence reviewed:
- Digifin terms: https://digifin.ghost.io/link-tyc-tarjeta-de-credito/
  - Product name: Tarjeta de Crédito Lemon Colateralizada en Crypto.
  - Physical credit card issued by Digifin S.A.
  - Visa network.
  - User grants a pledge over virtual assets forming the collateral deposit.
- Lemon launch statement: https://lemon.me/blog/tarjeta-cr%C3%A9dito-bitcoin
  - Published 2026-01-14, updated 2026-07-03.
  - Launches Argentina's Bitcoin-backed Visa credit card.
  - Initial model gives a peso credit limit backed by BTC without selling the BTC.
- Lemon help: https://help.lemon.me/es/articles/12742391-que-es-y-donde-puedo-usar-mi-lemon-credit-card
  - Current Argentina product identity and Visa acceptance.

Decision:
- Add active Lemon Bitcoin-backed Credit Card separately from prepaid Lemon Card.
- Reuse Digifin issuer and Visa network.
- Add 2026-01-14 launch event.
- Keep collateral description at product level; no separate collateral provider is created because the reviewed terms identify Digifin's pledge right rather than a distinct collateral-infrastructure provider.

## Takenos TakeCard

Accepted.

First-party evidence reviewed:
- Current TakeCard terms: https://help.takenos.com/en/articles/12103526-terminos-y-condiciones-para-takecard
  - USD prepaid card, virtual and physical.
  - Users also accept terms of provider Rain.
  - Card transactions debit the Takenos USD account balance.
  - The terms reviewed do not clearly identify Rain as regulated issuer, so no issuer role is inferred.
- Current product/help: https://help.takenos.com/en/articles/9718099-como-funciona-la-takecard
  - U.S.-issued international prepaid Visa card, virtual and physical.
  - Linked directly to Takenos account balance.
- Current landing page: https://takenos.com/tarjeta-internacional
  - Current TakeCard identity and payment directly from digital USD balance.

Decision:
- Add active Takenos TakeCard.
- Reuse Rain as `uses_provider`, not `issued_by`.
- Reuse Visa network.
- Do not invent an exact launch date from current help/terms; no lifecycle event is added until a dated first-party launch milestone is located.

## Rejected / unresolved claims

- Do not merge Lemon prepaid and Lemon credit into one record: they have different legal terms, payment models, and credit/collateral characteristics.
- Do not assign Rain an issuer role for TakeCard from provider wording alone.
- Do not create Bay Treasure Caramel as a card issuer; it is the crypto platform/VASP and conversion/custody entity in the prepaid terms, not the card issuer.
