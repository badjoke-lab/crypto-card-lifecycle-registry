# Phase 3 batch 004 review — 2026-08-30

## Candidate set

- Gemini Credit Card
- CL Card compatible with Ledger
- Crypto.com Prepaid Visa Card
- Wirex Card

## Accepted

### Gemini Credit Card

Accepted because Gemini primary sources establish a public launch date and the core infrastructure relationship at launch.

Primary evidence reviewed:
- https://www.gemini.com/fr/blog/apply-for-the-gemini-credit-card-today — 2022-04-14 public availability; Mastercard exclusive network partner; WebBank issuing bank.
- https://www.gemini.com/credit-card/btc — current product remains active and identifies WebBank as issuer.

Canonical promotion:
- program
- WebBank provider
- WebBank issuer relation
- Mastercard network relation
- 2022-04-14 launch event

### CL Card compatible with Ledger

Accepted with a deliberately narrow infrastructure model.

Primary evidence reviewed:
- https://www.ledger.com/cl-card — current Ledger product page identifies CL Card as provided by Baanx and describes region-dependent provider entities. It also states that funds transferred to the CL account are no longer self-custodial.
- https://www.ledger.com/blog-the-cl-card-comes-to-the-us — 2025-05-28 U.S. expansion statement identifies the U.S. CL Card as a Visa crypto card powered by Baanx.

Canonical promotion:
- program
- Baanx provider, typed only as `other` because the reviewed public wording says "provided by Baanx" and does not justify forcing a narrower issuer/program-manager role at group level
- unscoped Baanx provider relation
- U.S.-scoped Visa network relation
- 2025-05-28 U.S. expansion event

Unresolved and intentionally not generalized:
- exact issuer/legal-entity mapping across every current CL Card region
- exact network mapping outside the reviewed U.S. Visa evidence; other Ledger material also references Mastercard usage, so the U.S. Visa relationship must not be generalized globally

### Crypto.com Prepaid Visa Card

Accepted as a program distinct from the already-canonical U.S. Crypto.com Visa Signature Credit Card.

Primary evidence reviewed:
- https://crypto.com/document/us_card — U.S. cardholder agreement effective 2025-09-02 identifies Community Federal Savings Bank as issuer and Foris, Inc. as program manager.
- https://crypto.com/eea/levelup/basic — current EEA disclosure identifies Foris MT Limited as issuer/promoter under its Visa Principal Member issuing licence.
- https://crypto.com/ca/cards/ — current Canada disclosure identifies Digital Commerce Bank as issuer and Foris, Inc. as the offering entity.
- https://crypto.com/document/us_card_cro_locked_0 — historical U.S. agreement effective 2021-06-29 identifies Metropolitan Commercial Bank as issuer and Foris Inc. as program manager.

Canonical promotion:
- program
- Community Federal Savings Bank provider
- Foris, Inc. provider
- Foris MT Limited provider
- Digital Commerce Bank provider
- region-scoped current issuer/provider relations for U.S., EEA and Canada
- region-scoped Visa network relation across the reviewed regions
- `provider_changed` lifecycle event dated 2025-09-02 as an evidence milestone: the current U.S. agreement effective that date names CFSB, while the reviewed 2021 agreement named Metropolitan Commercial Bank

Uncertainty preserved:
- the reviewed documents establish that the U.S. issuer structure changed between the 2021 and 2025 agreements, but they do not establish the exact cutover day. The event description must say "changed by" the 2025 agreement effective date rather than claiming an exact operational migration timestamp.

## Deferred

### Wirex Card

Deferred, not rejected. Current Wirex primary material clearly establishes an active crypto card business and principal relationships with Visa/Mastercard, but the reviewed sources did not yet provide a sufficiently clean card-specific issuer/network mapping by consumer region to promote without over-generalizing infrastructure relationships.

## Batch boundary

- no rankings or reward comparisons
- no inferred provider roles
- no unscoped regional issuer/network generalization
- canonical enums remain machine-readable; presentation labels are separate UI concerns
- canonical validation must pass before merge
