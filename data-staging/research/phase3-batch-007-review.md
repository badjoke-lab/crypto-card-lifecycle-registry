# Phase 3 batch 007 review — discontinued card programs

Date: 2026-08-30

## Candidate set

- Binance Visa Debit Card (Europe/EEA)
- BlockFi Rewards Visa Signature Card

## Accepted

### Binance Visa Debit Card

Accepted as a discontinued historical program.

Evidence supports:
- the European Binance Visa Debit Card program existed;
- UAB Finansinės paslaugos „Contis“ was issuer;
- Visa was the card network;
- Binance confirmed the card would no longer be available from 2023-12-20;
- contemporaneous reporting reproduces the closure notice and quotes a Binance spokesperson.

Evidence limitation:
- the original Binance card support/terms pages are no longer available at their historical URLs in this review;
- therefore the canonical records use reputable contemporaneous reporting for the closure/issuer claim and set the new Binance records/evidence conservatively rather than treating a community repost as primary evidence.

### BlockFi Rewards Visa Signature Card

Accepted as a discontinued U.S. historical program.

Evidence supports:
- Evolve Bank & Trust as issuing bank;
- Deserve, Inc. as servicer;
- Visa network relationship;
- BlockFi's crypto-rewards layer was separate from the ordinary credit-card receivable;
- a BlockFi bankruptcy court filing states that Visa sent a termination letter on 2022-11-16 and that the BlockFi Rewards Visa was no longer active;
- Evolve subsequently stated that outstanding balances remained with Deserve and cardholders still had repayment obligations.

## Boundary decisions

- Binance community/forum reposts are not used as canonical evidence.
- Binance confidence is lower than the BlockFi package because the historical first-party page is no longer retrievable in this review.
- BlockFi card shutdown is recorded separately from BlockFi platform bankruptcy/withdrawal events; CCLR only records the card-program lifecycle fact.
- Post-shutdown servicing of outstanding BlockFi card balances is noted in scope text and is not represented as an active crypto-card program.
- No UI changes in this batch.
