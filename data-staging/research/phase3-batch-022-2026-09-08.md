# Phase 3 batch 022 research — 2026-09-08

## Candidate set

1. NORACARD
2. Zypto Premium Visa Card
3. Takenos SpicyCard

## Duplicate review

Canonical search before promotion returned no existing program records for NORACARD, Zypto or SpicyCard.

## Accepted

### NORACARD
- Current NORACARD terms describe a crypto-funded global card programme operated by Topplex Marketing Ltd.
- E-sign and card terms identify Third National, Inc. / Third National as issuer.
- Current public product terms describe Mastercard merchant acceptance; generic terms also define supported card networks as Visa or Mastercard, so the canonical network relation is limited to the current evidenced Mastercard product presentation rather than generalized to every possible programme variant.
- Exact original launch date was not established from reviewed first-party sources; no launch event is fabricated.

### Zypto Premium Visa Card
- Current Zypto product and help material describe reloadable physical and virtual Premium Visa cards funded by conversion of crypto into fiat at load time.
- Zypto card terms and GLBA/account-opening disclosures identify Third National as issuer.
- Visa Signature network/benefit identity is explicit in current first-party product and support material.
- Zypto's current restricted-jurisdictions disclosure records a lifecycle limitation effective 2026-03-12: new onboarding suspended in Missouri, Mississippi and Tennessee, with existing-card transition through 2026-04-27.

### Takenos SpicyCard
- SpicyCard is a separate Argentina-local prepaid Mastercard from Takenos, distinct from the international TakeCard already canonicalized.
- Current Takenos terms identify Pomelo Fintech Services S.A.S. as issuer.
- Terms explicitly state crypto selected by the user is converted to Argentine pesos for card spending; current help describes direct spending from the Takenos account balance.
- Exact initial launch date was not established; the 2025-05-22 terms publication is treated only as an evidence milestone.

## Rejected

None.

- NORACARD exact first operational launch date.
- Whether every NORACARD regional variant uses the same network; only current Mastercard presentation is promoted.
- Zypto Premium original global launch date.
- SpicyCard exact first operational launch date.

No unsupported issuer, launch or provider-role inference is added.
