### Bitpanda Card

Accepted with both current and historical issuer evidence.

Primary evidence reviewed:
- https://www.bitpanda.com/en/card — current Visa card; current page identifies Transact Payments Malta Limited (TPML) as issuer under a Visa Europe licence and limits ordering to eligible euro-area residents.
- https://cdn.bitpanda.com/terms-and-conditions/bitpanda-card-bitpanda-en-latest.pdf — Bitpanda Card Product Terms v2.0.0 dated 2025-04-01 identify TPML as Card Issuer and Visa scheme membership.
- https://support.bitpanda.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018933120-What-is-the-Bitpanda-Card-and-what-are-its-benefits- — Bitpanda states the old card was deactivated on 2025-03-31 and the new Bitpanda Card could be activated from 2025-04-01 08:00.
- https://cdn.bitpanda.com/terms-and-conditions/contis-account-bitpanda-en-latest.pdf — historical card-account terms identify UAB “Finansinės paslaugos „Contis“” as issuer/service provider of the prior Bitpanda Visa debit card.

Canonical promotion:
- Bitpanda Card program
- historical Contis/Solaris issuer provider
- historical issuer relation ending 2025-03-31
- current TPML issuer relation starting 2025-04-01
- Visa network relation
- 2025-04-01 provider-change lifecycle event

Boundary:
- the transition date is grounded in Bitpanda's explicit old-card deactivation/new-card activation dates, not inferred from document timestamps alone.
- provider status for the historical Contis legal entity is left `unknown`; the evidence establishes the historical issuing role, not its current corporate lifecycle state.

### Uphold Visa Card — United States

Accepted as the current U.S. Uphold card program, with historical infrastructure recorded as a lifecycle change rather than an undated concurrent relation.

Primary evidence reviewed:
- https://uphold.com/en-us/legal/card-terms — current agreement, last updated 2025-09-16, identifies the Uphold Essential/Elite cards collectively as the Uphold Visa Card, issued by Cross River Bank on Visa, with Uphold HQ Inc. as Program Manager.
- https://cdn.uphold.com/disclosures/cardholder-agreement-us.pdf — archived U.S. agreement identifies the earlier Uphold debit card as issued by Metropolitan Commercial Bank under Mastercard.

Canonical promotion:
- Uphold Visa Card program
- Cross River Bank issuer provider
- Uphold HQ Inc. program-manager provider
- current U.S. issuer/program-manager/Visa relations
- 2025-09-16 `infrastructure_changed` evidence milestone comparing current CRB/Visa terms against the archived MCB/Mastercard agreement

Boundary:
- the 2025-09-16 date is the current agreement's last-updated evidence milestone; it does not claim the exact operational cutover occurred that day.
- historical MCB/Mastercard relationships are not added as open-ended relations because the exact end date was not established by the reviewed evidence.
- this U.S. program is not generalized to older or current Uphold cards in other jurisdictions.

## Batch boundary
