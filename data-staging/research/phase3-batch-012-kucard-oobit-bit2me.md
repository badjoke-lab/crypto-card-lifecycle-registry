# Phase 3 batch 012 review — KuCard, Oobit and Bit2Me

Date: 2026-08-31

## Candidate set

- KuCard
- Oobit Crypto Card
- Bit2Me Card
- KuCard partner UAB EURLITA
- Oobit issuer ThirdNational
- Bit2Me issuer/manager PECUNIA CARDS EDE, S.L.U. (Pecunpay)

## Accepted

### KuCard

KuCoin's official KuCard terms identify KuCard as a Visa debit card issued by Wallester AS and identify UAB EURLITA as the Partner providing KuCard services on Wallester's behalf. KuCoin's November 2023 launch material describes the official KuCard launch for EEA users.

Wallester already exists in canonical data. UAB EURLITA is added only as a service-provider dependency; it is not labeled issuer.

### Oobit Crypto Card

Oobit's current card terms identify the program as a Visa card and explicitly define the issuer for the reviewed program as ThirdNational. Oobit's current product page describes wallet-connected crypto spending at Visa merchants. Oobit also announced a U.S. launch on 2025-12-08 for wallet-native crypto payments.

The legal entity behind the issuer name is not expanded beyond the exact `ThirdNational` name used in the reviewed Oobit terms.

### Bit2Me Card

Bit2Me's official legal terms identify Bit2Me Card as a Mastercard and PECUNIA CARDS EDE, S.L.U. / Pecunpay as issuer and manager of the card/payment account. Bit2Me's official launch post records the product launch on 2023-01-17.

## Boundary decisions

- no UI changes
- no inferred issuer role for UAB EURLITA
- no expansion of the Oobit issuer beyond the exact legal name disclosed in current terms
- Oobit's 2025-12-08 event is a U.S. market launch, not the global origin date of the whole card program
- Bit2Me's card-account manager role is represented separately from Mastercard network use
- rewards/cashback are not canonicalized except where incidentally present in source titles
