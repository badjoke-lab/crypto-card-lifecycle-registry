## Accepted

### MEXC Global Card

**Decision:** accept for canonical promotion.

First-party MEXC material identifies the MEXC Global Card as a distinct Visa Platinum virtual card launched on 2026-08-31, separate from the older MEXC Card (APAC) and the MEXC × ether.fi card. MEXC states that the card is funded with USDT and settles purchases over Visa's network.

Evidence:
- MEXC launch release (2026-08-31): https://blog.mexc.com/press-release/mexc-launches-mexc-global-card-with-up-to-10-cashback-and-7-annualized-returns/
- MEXC product guide: https://www.mexc.com/learn/article/what-is-the-mexc-global-card-cashback-7-apr-fees-limits-and-how-to-apply/1
- MEXC comparison guide establishing that Global Card is a separate product from MEXC Card (APAC): https://www.mexc.com/learn/article/mexc-global-card-vs-mexc-card-apac-vs-mexc-ether-fi-card-which-crypto-visa-card-is-right-for-you/1

**Canonical-safe claims:** active program; launch date 2026-08-31; Visa network; virtual Visa Platinum card; distinct from MEXC Card (APAC).

**Unresolved:** the reviewed first-party material says MEXC issues the card directly, but this review does not yet have a legal/cardholder agreement naming the precise regulated issuing entity. Do not create a separate issuer provider or legal-entity relation until that evidence is obtained.

### Bleap Card

**Decision:** accept for canonical promotion.

Bleap's current cardholder terms explicitly define the product as a Mastercard debit card linked to the self-custodial Bleap Wallet. The terms identify Unlimit EU Ltd as the issuer and Mastercard as the scheme/network. Bleap's first-party launch article dated 2025-04-16 describes the Mastercard partnership and card launch.

Evidence:
- Bleap Cardholder Terms (EEA), updated 2026-05-25: https://www.bleap.finance/en-us/legal-agreements/bleap-cardholder-terms-eea
- Bleap and Mastercard launch article (2025-04-16): https://www.bleap.finance/en-us/blog/bleap-and-mastercard
- Current Bleap card page: https://www.bleap.finance/en-us/card/crypto-card

**Canonical-safe claims:** active program; Mastercard network; issued by Unlimit EU Ltd; non-custodial wallet linkage; launch/partnership announced 2025-04-16.

### Gate US Card

**Decision:** accept for canonical promotion as the U.S.-scoped Gate card program, not as an unscoped global Gate Card record.

Gate US announced the card on 2026-07-10. First-party sources identify Lead Bank as issuer, Visa as network, Bridge as program manager/card-services infrastructure, and Stripe/Bridge as stablecoin infrastructure. Availability is explicitly limited to eligible users in selected U.S. states, so relations and the launch event must retain U.S. scope rather than being generalized globally.

Evidence:
- Gate US launch announcement (2026-07-10): https://www.gate.com/en-us/announcements/article/45495
- Gate US product page/card disclaimer: https://www.gate.com/en-us/card
- Gate US architecture explainer: https://www.gate.com/en-us/blog/gate-us-card-explained-how-usdc-moves-from-an-on-chain-asset-to-global-everyday-payments

**Canonical-safe claims:** active U.S.-scoped program; launch date 2026-07-10; issued by Lead Bank; Visa network; Bridge program-management/card-services role; stablecoin settlement infrastructure involving Bridge and Stripe where stated.
