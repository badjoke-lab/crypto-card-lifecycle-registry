# Phase 3 batch 010 review — current major cards

Date: 2026-08-30

## Candidate set

- WhiteBIT Nova
- Bitget Card
- Xapo Card
- RedotPay Card (reviewed, deferred from this bounded batch because the current terms intentionally abstract the named BIN sponsor behind a generic BIN Sponsor definition)

## Accepted

### WhiteBIT Nova

WhiteBIT's current help material identifies Nova as a Visa debit card issued and provided by Wallester AS, an Estonian payment institution and Visa Principal Member. WhiteBIT also documents a card reissuance process beginning 2026-03-24 for users with verified Ukrainian addresses.

### Bitget Card

Bitget's APAC terms identify the Bitget Card as a Visa card issued through DCS Card Centre Pte. Ltd. Bitget's official 2026-04-02 launch statement describes the APAC rollout with Visa and DCS. On 2026-08-01 Bitget moved new card applications to Bitget Wallet; existing Bitget Cards continue to work, but replacement after expiry is no longer available. The program is therefore `limited`, not `discontinued`.

Only the reviewed APAC issuer relationship is promoted in this batch. Other Bitget Card regional/legal generations are not generalized into an unscoped issuer relation.

### Xapo Card

Xapo's current support documentation describes the Xapo Card as a global debit card tied to Xapo membership and capable of spending from USD and/or Bitcoin-linked balances. Xapo's transaction support material explicitly describes Xapo Bank as the issuing bank. Xapo also documents Visa and Mastercard card-network variants; network relations are recorded without asserting that every holder receives both variants.

## Boundary decisions

- no UI changes
- Bitget's 2026-08-01 change is recorded as a service limitation/migration of new applications, not a shutdown of existing cards
- no Bitget issuer relationship is generalized beyond the APAC evidence reviewed here
- WhiteBIT's Ukraine-address reissuance is a regional lifecycle event, not a global provider-change event
- Xapo Visa and Mastercard are network variants; the registry does not imply dual-network operation for a single card
- RedotPay remains a follow-up candidate because its current terms define the issuer as a variable `BIN Sponsor` without naming the sponsor in the reviewed terms
