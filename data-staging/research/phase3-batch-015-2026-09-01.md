### 1. LBank Card — current 2026 program / generation boundary under review

First-party evidence:

- LBank, `LBank Card Is Now Live, Unlocking a New Global Crypto Payment Experience`, 2026-06-26: https://www.lbank.com/support/articles/2070438941353312256
- LBank, `LBank Card Physical Card Is Now Officially Open for Applications`, 2026-08-24: https://www.lbank.info/ro/support/articles/2091715794642665472
- LBank, `Announcement on the Sunset of LBank Card Service`, 2025-05-28: https://support.lbank.com/hc/en-gb/articles/47464483879193-Announcement-on-the-Sunset-of-LBank-Card-Service
- LBank, `LBank Visa Card(441112) User Agreement`, 2025-04-19: https://support.lbank.com/hc/en-gb/articles/30057650406681-LBank-Visa-Card-441112-User-Agreement

Supported claims:

- LBank announced the current LBank Card as live on 2026-06-26.
- The current card uses the Visa acceptance network and can spend from the user's LBank spot balance.
- Physical-card applications opened on 2026-08-24 for eligible users, with Visa merchant and supported ATM use.
- A prior LBank Card service was explicitly sunset beginning 2025-06-05.

Boundary to resolve before canonical promotion:

- Do not silently collapse the 2025 sunset program and the 2026 live program into one continuous lifecycle without evidence that they are the same legal/program generation.
- Conversely, do not create a second program merely because the service relaunched if first-party terms identify continuity.
- Precise current issuer/program-manager identity remains unresolved from the launch notices alone.

Disposition: **accepted for bounded review, canonical identity decision pending**.

### 2. MetaMask Card — U.S. general availability and subsequent signup pause

First-party evidence:

- MetaMask, `MetaMask and Mastercard partner to launch the US MetaMask Card, with first-time availability in New York`, 2026-02-26: https://metamask.io/en-GB/news/metamask-and-mastercard-partner-to-launch-the-us-metamask-card
- MetaMask Help Center, `What is MetaMask Card?`: https://support.metamask.io/manage-crypto/metamask-card/what-is-metamask-card

Supported claims:

- U.S. general availability was announced on 2026-02-26, including New York.
- The U.S. card is powered by Baanx/Monavate, uses Mastercard, and is issued by Cross River Bank according to the launch announcement.
- MetaMask later temporarily paused new Card sign-ups in the United States; existing cardholders were stated to be unaffected.

Canonical modeling direction:

- This is not a new program candidate because `MetaMask Card` already exists in canonical.
- Candidate additions are a U.S. jurisdiction expansion / launch event and a later `card_service_limited`-style event for new-signup availability, while preserving that existing cardholders remained unaffected.
- Provider relations should only be changed if current canonical relations do not already represent the evidenced Baanx/Monavate, Mastercard, and Cross River roles.

Disposition: **accepted for lifecycle review**.

### 3. Binance Card Brazil — Mastercard generation

First-party evidence:

- Mastercard Newsroom, `Binance e Mastercard lançam cartão de cripto no Brasil`, 2025-10-01: https://newsroom.mastercard.com/news/latin-america/pt-br/noticias/comunicados-de-imprensa/pr-pt/2025/outubro/binance-e-mastercard-lancam-cartao-de-cripto-no-brasil/
- Mastercard, `Crypto Card Program` market examples: https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/business/payments/consumer-payments/next-gen-payments/digital-asset-solutions/crypto-card-program.html

Supported claims:

- Binance and Mastercard launched Binance Card for users in Brazil on 2025-10-01.
- The product uses Mastercard and converts supported crypto assets to Brazilian real for payment.
- This is materially distinct from the historical Binance Visa Debit Card Europe already represented in canonical by network and jurisdiction.

Canonical modeling direction:

- Treat as a separate program generation/canonical program unless evidence shows the Brazil Mastercard product is merely the same legal program migrated from the Europe Visa product.
- Preserve Brazil jurisdiction scope.
- Do not infer issuer or processor beyond first-party evidence found in the bounded review.

Disposition: **accepted for canonical-program review**.
