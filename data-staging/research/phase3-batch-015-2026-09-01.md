# Phase 3 batch 015 research — 2026-09-01

## Bounded candidate set

This batch continues Phase 3 reviewed record growth. It mixes new card programs with material lifecycle/jurisdiction changes to an existing program. Canonical promotion is limited to claims supported by reviewed first-party sources.

### 1. LBank Card — accepted as one lifecycle record with an explicit interruption

First-party evidence:

- LBank, `Announcement on the Sunset of LBank Card Service`, 2025-05-28: https://support.lbank.com/hc/en-gb/articles/47464483879193-Announcement-on-the-Sunset-of-LBank-Card-Service
- LBank, `LBank Card Is Now Live, Unlocking a New Global Crypto Payment Experience`, 2026-06-26: https://www.lbank.com/support/articles/2070438941353312256
- LBank, `LBank Card Physical Card Is Now Officially Open for Applications`, 2026-08-24: https://www.lbank.info/ro/support/articles/2091715794642665472

Supported claims:

- LBank explicitly sunset the application and spending functions of the then-current LBank Card from 2025-06-05.
- LBank explicitly announced `LBank Card` live again on 2026-06-26.
- The 2026 card spends from the user's LBank spot balance and supports Visa merchants worldwide.
- Physical-card applications opened in August 2026.

Canonical decision:

- Model `LBank Card` as one canonical program with a documented service interruption and later relaunch rather than inventing an unproven new legal generation.
- The record must not imply uninterrupted service between the 2025 sunset and the 2026 relaunch.
- Precise regulated issuer/program-manager identity remains unresolved and is not inferred.
- The separate one-time-use `LBank Prepaid Card` launched in March 2026 is a different product and is outside this batch.

Disposition: **accepted for canonical promotion**.

### 2. MetaMask Card — U.S. general availability and subsequent signup pause

First-party evidence:

- MetaMask, `MetaMask and Mastercard partner to launch the US MetaMask Card, with first-time availability in New York`, 2026-02-26: https://metamask.io/en-GB/news/metamask-and-mastercard-partner-to-launch-the-us-metamask-card
- MetaMask Help Center, `What is MetaMask Card?`: https://support.metamask.io/manage-crypto/metamask-card/what-is-metamask-card

Supported claims:

- MetaMask announced U.S. general availability on 2026-02-26, including New York for the first time.
- The U.S. program is powered by Baanx (now Monavate), uses Mastercard, and is issued by Cross River Bank.
- MetaMask later temporarily paused new Card sign-ups in the United States; existing cardholders were stated to be unaffected.
- The Help Center separately records the Metal Card order pause from 2026-06-02, which already exists in canonical.

Canonical decision:

- Do not create a new program: `MetaMask Card` already exists.
- Add the U.S. GA event and the U.S. new-signup limitation as separate lifecycle events.
- Add U.S.-scoped Cross River Bank issuer and Baanx/Monavate provider relations only where not already represented; retain Mastercard as the network.
- Do not rewrite the Metal Card pause into a full-program shutdown.

Disposition: **accepted for canonical lifecycle/relationship promotion**.

### 3. Binance Card Brazil — Mastercard generation

First-party evidence:

- Mastercard Newsroom, `Binance e Mastercard lançam cartão de cripto no Brasil`, 2025-10-01: https://www.mastercard.com/news/latin-america/pt-br/noticias/comunicados-de-imprensa/pr-pt/2025/outubro/binance-e-mastercard-lancam-cartao-de-cripto-no-brasil/

Supported claims:

- Binance and Mastercard launched Binance Card for users in Brazil on 2025-10-01.
- The card uses Mastercard and converts supported crypto assets to Brazilian real at payment time.
- The source supports Brazil availability and Mastercard network use, but does not establish a regulated issuer or processor that can safely be promoted here.

Canonical decision:

- Create a separate Brazil Mastercard program record rather than attach it to the discontinued European Binance Visa Debit Card generation.
- Scope the launch and network relationship to Brazil.
- Leave issuer and processor unresolved rather than infer them.

Disposition: **accepted for canonical promotion**.
